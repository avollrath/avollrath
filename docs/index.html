Codex Hue Notifier – Turning My Desk Lamp into an AI Status Light

While working with Codex in VS Code I often start a task and then look away from the screen. So I connected the Codex notify hook to a Philips Hue lamp and turned it into a tiny AI status light that flashes whenever Codex finishes.

About

Hi, I'm André Vollrath, a UI/UX designer and front-end developer based in Helsinki. With a background in digital and print media design and over ten years of experience working in agencies and SaaS teams, I focus on designing clear interfaces for digital products and bringing them to life in the browser.


I enjoy simplifying complex systems and turning them into intuitive user experiences. My work combines visual design, UX thinking, and front-end development, allowing me to collaborate closely with engineers and ensure that ideas translate smoothly from concept to working software.
