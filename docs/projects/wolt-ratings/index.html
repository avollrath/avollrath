Wolt Ratings - Order History Tracker

Wolt Ratings exists because Wolt gives you no way to remember which venues were worth reordering from or which dishes disappointed you - your order history is just a receipt archive with no memory.

It's a Chrome extension that captures your session token and syncs your full order history to a local Flask backend, paired with a dashboard where you can search, filter, rate orders with stars, and attach personal notes. Clicking a venue opens a modal with per-restaurant stats, your most ordered items, and a full visit history.

Everything stays local - no accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Orders are stored in a plain JSON file you can import and export freely.
